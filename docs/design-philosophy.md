# Design Philosophy of Cricket247

## Principles

- Since the app is all about cricket, every layout, visual elements, typography, and interaction should resemble the sport in some way. The design choice should be intentional, not template-driven.
- Visual Hierarchy is paramount.
- No unnecessary cards in cards mess.
- Design choices have to feel native mobile app like, as the app is primarily used on mobile devices.
- The elements should come to life on interactions, with subtle animations and micro-interactions that enhance the user experience without being distracting.

### Layout

- Responsive design is crucial, for mobile and tablet views, interactive elements should be touch and gesture friendly, with larger tap targets and intuitive navigation.
- Desktop view can leverage the larger screen by having asymmetrical two column layout.
- Ample whitespace in text heavy pages, App like layout on interactive pages.

### Visual Elements

- Bring cricket relevant svg icons to clarify the content and make it more engaging.
- Use a color palette that reflects the vibrancy of cricket, with a balance of bold and neutral tones to create a visually appealing and cohesive design.
- Use typography that is clear and legible, with a mix of styles to create visual interest and hierarchy. Consider using a combination of serif and sans-serif fonts that have a sporty feel to them, while still being easy to read.
